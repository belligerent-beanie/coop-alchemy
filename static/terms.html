Terms of use

Co-op Alchemy is a small game run by one person for friends and their friends. By creating an account or playing you agree to the rules below. They are short on purpose.

Who can play

  • The game is meant for players aged 13 or over. If you are younger, ask a parent or guardian to play with you.
  • Signup is by invitation. Do not share an invite code on a public page: each one works once, and the administrators can see which invite an account used.
  • One person, one account. Do not make accounts to get around a removal.
  • Some of the players you meet in a game may be computer-controlled.

How to behave

  • Be kind. No harassment, hate, threats, or sexual content, in names or in anything else you type.
  • Usernames, game names and recipe names are visible to other players. Do not use your real name, a phone number, an address, or anyone else's name. Names containing blocked words are refused.
  • Do not try to break, overload or cheat the game, or to read other people's data. If you find a hole, tell the person below instead of using it.
  • Do not impersonate other people or the administrators.

Moderation

  • Any player can use the ⚑ button beside another player's name to report them. The report goes to the administrators and does not say who sent it.
  • The administrators may end a game, warn a player, disable an account or delete it, at any time and without notice, if the rules are broken or the game is being misused.

What you give the game

  • Your recipe ideas are yours. If you promote a recipe on the Create Recipes page, you let the administrators add it to the game for everyone, with your username as credit. Once it is in the recipe book it stays unless they remove it.
  • What the game stores about you, and for how long, is on the privacy page. You can change your password or delete your account yourself from Account on the home page.

No promises

  • The game is provided as it is, for fun and for free. It may be slow, change, lose a saved game, or be switched off. Saved games are only kept for 3 days.
  • The person who runs this server is not responsible for what other players type or do, but will act on reports.
  • These rules may change. The page you are reading is always the current version.
