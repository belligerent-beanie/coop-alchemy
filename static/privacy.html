Privacy

This game keeps as little about you as it can. Here is everything it stores, and for how long.

What is stored

  • Your username and a scrambled form of your password (a salted hash). The password itself is never stored and nobody can read it.
  • Your games: the elements you discover and which player found each one. A game you save is kept for 3 days. Games you do not save disappear when everyone leaves.
  • Recipe drafts and proposals you make on the Create Recipes page, until you delete them.
  • Daily challenge scores and attempts, for up to 400 days.
  • Solo mode progress: which elements you have found in Solo mode, whether you have found them all (which opens Create Recipes), the achievements you have earned and when, and the day you last used Solo's daily hint. It is kept until you delete your account.
  • Your login lives in memory on the server and in your browser's storage on this device. It ends after 24 hours without use or when you log out. There are no cookies and no tracking.
  • An activity log on the server records sign-ups, logins (including failed ones), password changes and account deletions, with your username and network address. It is used to stop abuse and is deleted after 14 days.
  • Reports about you: if another player reports you, the administrators see your username, what the report is about, a short note if one was written, the name of the game, how many reports there are and when. Nothing records who sent a report. A report is deleted after 14 days, or when an administrator dismisses it.
  • An email address only if the person running this server asks for one to confirm accounts; it is used for that single confirmation message.

Names

Usernames, game names and the names of recipes you create are checked against a list of blocked words when you choose them. The administrators can also disable or delete an account that causes problems.

What is not stored or shared

No analytics, no advertising, no profiles, and nothing is sold or given to anyone. If the sign-up form shows a "human check", that check is run by Cloudflare (Turnstile), which then sees your browser and network address while the form is open.

Your choices

  • Change your password any time from "Account" on the home page.
  • Delete your account from the same place. That removes your account, drafts, daily scores and Solo progress, deletes saved games nobody else played, and replaces your name in saved games you shared with "deleted player". Reports about you are deleted too. It cannot be undone. Credit on a recipe the administrators already added to the game's recipe book is part of that book and stays unless they remove it. The activity log is not edited; it expires on its own after 14 days.
